01

The leads

The two people every other name on this list eventually connects back to.

Jason Duval

Official
Playable protagonist

"Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder."

Grew up around grifters and crooks, then did a stint in the Army trying to shake off a troubled adolescence. Ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners — exactly the kind of life he was hoping to leave behind. Ex-military, and not much for conversation; meeting Lucia could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him, and even he isn't sure which yet.

Read the story →

Lucia Caminos

Official
Playable protagonist

"The only thing that matters is who you know and what you got."

Taught to fight by her father almost as soon as she could walk, and life's been swinging at her ever since — fighting to protect her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, and sheer luck got her back out. Fresh out of prison and committed to smart moves only from here, she's chasing the good life her mother once dreamed of back in Liberty City, whatever that ends up costing.

02

Circle & contacts

People Jason and Lucia already know, or come to trust, outside of any one job.

Brian Heder

Official
Smuggler, Jason's landlord

"Looks like a Leonida beach bum — moves like a great white shark."

A classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys, still moving product through his boatyard with his third wife, Lori — and long past the point of doing his own dirty work. Lets Jason live rent-free at one of his properties in exchange for handling local shakedowns, plus the occasional obligatory glass of Lori's sangria. Warns Jason about Lucia's growing recklessness using a story about a friend, Jimmy Kanto, who died refusing to slow down a runaway carousel ride as a kid.

Read the story →

Lori

Brian's wife & business partner

Brian's third wife, and no mere bystander — she works the business alongside him and appears to know everything that's going on. Present for, and clearly paying close attention to, the conversation where Jason and Lucia's trouble first comes to light.

Read the story →

Raul Bautista

Official
Veteran bank robber

"Experience counts."

Confidence, charm, and cunning — a seasoned bank robber always hunting for talent willing to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards. Tests Jason and Lucia on an actual bank job, then takes a particular shine to Lucia, treating her almost as a protégé. His own recklessness raises the stakes with every score, to the point that his crew will eventually have to double down or walk away entirely.

Read the story →

Cal Hampton

Official
Jason's friend

"What if everything on the internet was true?"

Jason's friend and a fellow associate of Brian's. Feels safest at home, snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers and some private browser tabs open — happily paranoid, and at the low tide of America exactly where he wants to be, even as Jason clearly has bigger plans. Delivers the closing line of the extended look, telling Jason and Lucia to enjoy the present because these are their glory days.

03

The music scene

Boobie Ike's other business — a record label built around his young protégé and the act carrying it.

Boobie Ike

Official
Owner, Jack of Hearts strip club

"It's all about heart — the Jack of Hearts."

A local Vice City legend who acts like it — one of the few to turn time in the streets into a legitimate empire spanning real estate, a strip club, and a recording studio. All smiles until it's time to talk business, and most invested in his recording-studio partnership with Dre'Quan Priest, Only Raw Records, now that they finally have a potential hit act on their hands. An early mission giver, sending Jason and Lucia to collect a package from Raymond rather than going himself.

Read the story →

Dre'Quan Priest

Official
Label boss, Only Raw Records

"You're with the label now."

Always more of a hustler than a gangster, even back when he was dealing on the streets just to get by — breaking into music was the real goal the whole time. Now that he's signed Real Dimez, his days booking acts into Boobie's strip club may be numbered as he sets his sights on the wider Vice City scene.

Real Dimez

Official
Rap duo — Bae-Luxe & Roxy

"All my dimes in this club. Meet my twin, make it a dub."

Friends since high school, Bae-Luxe and Roxy turned a history of shaking down local dealers into cold, hard cash through spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence. An early hit single with local rapper DWNPLY took them to new heights; five years and a lot of trouble later, they're signed to Only Raw Records, hoping lightning strikes twice.

Threats

The people actively working against Jason and Lucia — confirmed and implied.

Mendez

Corrupt task force cop

Leads a dirty police task force that protects its own no matter the cost. First crosses paths with Jason and Lucia after they stumble onto evidence in an abandoned warehouse, then again during the Raymond shootout — by the end of which he knows exactly who they are. Reads as an early-game antagonist rather than the story's final one.

Higher circles

The people Jason and Lucia meet once the jobs start getting bigger — including at least one face carried over from GTA Online.

Well-connected fixer

Previously seen in GTA Online's Moneyfronts storyline. Brings Jason and Lucia into a much higher-stakes job — bodyguarding Andre Dan Leon — signaling how far they've come from robbing local stores.

Mr. Faber

GTA Online connection

A powerful, thoroughly dishonest billionaire from GTA Online with ties to Valentina and, by extension, wealthy figures across Los Santos. Hasn't appeared directly in GTA 6 footage — his link to this story, if any, is still unconfirmed.

Wealthy client

The target of Jason and Lucia's bodyguard job at Mega Mundo's headquarters party. Skips his own event to do drugs upstairs with an associate who turns out to be working against him, and is left stranded during the ambush when the account he's accused of controlling turns out to already be empty.
